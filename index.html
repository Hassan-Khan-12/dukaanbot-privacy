DukaanBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

DukaanBot is a WhatsApp assistant that helps small businesses in Karachi take orders by text or voice note. We use your messages only to answer you and handle your order. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

What DukaanBot is

DukaanBot answers customer questions and records orders for participating craft and service businesses over WhatsApp. It understands English and Urdu, including voice notes. It was developed by Muhammad Hassan Khan as a final-year project at Iqra University, Karachi.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • The text messages and voice notes you send to a business through DukaanBot.
  • Order details you give us, such as items, quantities, delivery address and preferred time.
  • Basic message information, such as when a message was sent and whether it was delivered.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and confirm your orders.
  • To turn your voice notes into text so the assistant can understand them.
  • To pass your order to the business you are ordering from.
  • To find and fix errors in the service.

Who else handles your data

Messages travel through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform, which has its own privacy policy. To transcribe voice notes and understand messages, DukaanBot sends their content to the speech and language services it uses. Our hosting provider stores the data on our behalf. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it with anyone else except the business you are ordering from.

How long we keep it

We keep conversations and order details only as long as needed to complete your order, give support and fix problems. After that we delete them.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, send “delete my data” to DukaanBot on WhatsApp, or email us at the address below from the number or email you used. We will delete your conversations and order details and confirm when it’s done.

Children

DukaanBot is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how DukaanBot handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
